1. BACKGROUND

   Independent bookshops face increasing competition from large
   online retailers and e-readers. A strong digital presence
   helps small bookshops reach readers beyond their physical
   neighbourhood, promote curated selections and build a
   loyal community of readers.

2. PROBLEM STATEMENT

   The bookshop currently has no dedicated website. Customers
   cannot:
     - Browse books by genre from home
     - Search for a title or author online
     - See price and availability before visiting
     - Contact the shop or check opening hours easily

3. PROPOSED SOLUTION

   Build a simple, responsive website prototype that:
     - Introduces the shop and its curation philosophy
     - Displays books by genre with cover, title, author,
       description and price
     - Provides live search and category filter
     - Highlights Staff Picks to showcase personality
     - Includes Reader Reviews and Contact sections
     - Works on desktop, tablet and mobile

4. EXPECTED BENEFITS

   - Improved online presence and discoverability
   - Easier browsing and pre-visit planning for customers
   - Central point for contact details and opening hours
   - Foundation for future e-commerce features
   - Practical portfolio evidence of project management skill

5. COST SUMMARY

   Estimated cost   : LKR 500 (small reserve)
   Actual cost      : LKR 0

   All tools used are free: HTML/CSS/JavaScript, VS Code,
   Git, GitHub, Figma free plan.

6. RISK SUMMARY

   Key risks:
     - Delayed shop information (R01)
     - Unapproved branding and images (R03)
     - Scope expansion (R04)
     - Timeline slippage (R08)

   Full details in docs/08-Risk-Register.txt.

7. RECOMMENDATION

   Proceed with the four-week bookshop website prototype using
   an Agile-inspired approach. Focus on delivering a working,
   tested prototype rather than a full e-commerce platform.
