1. BUSINESS REQUIREMENTS

   BR-01  Provide a professional online presence for the shop.      Must
   BR-02  Showcase books by genre with descriptive info.            Must
   BR-03  Make contact details and hours easy to find.              Must
   BR-04  Support mobile and tablet visitors.                       Must
   BR-05  Allow future expansion to e-commerce features.            Could

2. FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS

   FR-01  Homepage with shop introduction and navigation menu.      Must
   FR-02  Promotional hero section.                                 Must
   FR-03  Search bar (by title or author).                          Must
   FR-04  Book catalogue grouped by genre.                          Must
   FR-05  Each book shows cover, title, author, description, price. Must
   FR-06  Category filter buttons.                                  Must
   FR-07  Staff Picks section showcasing curated books.             Should
   FR-08  Reader Reviews section (sample reviews).                  Should
   FR-09  Contact section with address, hours, phone, email.        Must
   FR-10  Footer with copyright and links.                          Must
   FR-11  Save/Bookmark heart on each book.                         Could

3. NON-FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS

   NFR-01  Responsive on desktop, tablet and mobile.                Must
   NFR-02  Page loads quickly on common connections.                Must
   NFR-03  Clear, readable typography and colour contrast.          Must
   NFR-04  Consistent warm bookshop visual style.                   Must
   NFR-05  Accessible: alt text, semantic HTML, keyboard nav.       Should
   NFR-06  Cross-browser: Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari.            Must
   NFR-07  Maintainable: organised folder structure.                Should

4. CONTENT REQUIREMENTS (TO BE CONFIRMED)

   - Shop name, logo, branding
   - Book catalogue with covers and prices
   - Contact info (address, hours, phone, email)
   - Social media links
   - Owner's curated picks

   Note: any unverified content uses placeholder text or images
   and is clearly marked as SAMPLE.

5. ASSUMPTIONS

   - Shop information will be provided or confirmed in Week 1-2.
   - Books can be displayed without online payment.
   - All development tools are available free of charge.
